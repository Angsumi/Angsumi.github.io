Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026. This notice describes information used to respond to research inquiries.

1. Introduction

Angsumi ("we," "us," or "our") operates https://angsumi.online and its research inquiry service. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, read research resources or submit project inquiry forms.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when submitting inquiries or using our services:

  • Contact Details: Name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number provided in contact forms.
  • Project Requirements: Research question, dataset type, sample count, institution, timing and budget you choose to provide in the inquiry form.
  • Technical Data: If you accept analytics, Google Analytics may process your browser and device information, page titles and paths, referring page, and a browser identifier stored in analytics cookies. Our tag removes URL query strings from the page URLs it sends.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for legitimate business purposes:

  • To respond to your project inquiries and provide tailored technical blueprints and cost estimates.
  • To scope and deliver agreed bioinformatics and scientific analysis services.
  • To operate, maintain, and optimize our website speed and security.
  • To protect against fraudulent inquiries or malicious security attempts.

4. Zero Selling of Personal Data

We never sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information to third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks. Your contact details are used solely to communicate directly with you regarding your projects.

5. Cookies & Analytics

Our website stores your analytics choice locally. Google Analytics loads only after you select Accept analytics. If you reject analytics, our Google Analytics tag does not load. You can change your choice using the Privacy choices control on the site. When accepted, Google Analytics uses first-party cookies to measure visits and fixed research inquiry and resource events; our tag configures those cookies to expire after 30 days and does not intentionally send names, email addresses, form entries, or URL query strings. Advertising consent remains denied. Google may process analytics data under its Privacy Policy.

6. Third-Party Services & Form Processing

Our guided inquiry form stores leads through Supabase and the configured transactional notification provider. Historical inquiry and workshop forms used Formspree; and Google Analytics for site measurement only when you opt in. Guided inquiry data is sent to the lead service, not intentionally to Google Analytics. Each provider processes information under its own terms and privacy policy.

7. Data Security & Retention

The inquiry service uses access controls and separates public forms from owner access; do not include confidential research data in the public form. We retain inquiry information only as long as necessary to fulfill project requirements or comply with legal obligations.

8. Your Rights (DPDP Act & GDPR)

In accordance with the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act and global privacy standards, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of your personal data stored with us at any time.

Structured research inquiries

The guided form stores contact details, research scope, optional context, consent version, a coarse source label and operational records such as status and owner notes. Supabase hosts the configured lead API and database. Transactional notification delivery is separate from saving the inquiry.

Ask through the published contact route about access, correction, deletion and the retention arrangements applicable to your inquiry.
