From question to handover.

  1. Feasibility review: describe your question and input stage without sending sensitive files.
  2. Written scope and quote: agree methods, outputs, costs, milestones and acceptance checks.
  3. Analysis checkpoints: review data quality, design choices and interpretation as agreed.
  4. Handover: receive the agreed outputs, documentation and support arrangements.

Practical questions before you begin.

What should I send first?

Your research question, assay or data type, approximate sample count, study groups and current stage. Do not upload participant identifiers or raw confidential data.

What will it cost, and how long will it take?

Input readiness, design, data scale, compute, interpretation and revision needs determine the quote and milestones. A short inquiry is enough to start the scope discussion.

Will the results be significant or publishable?

No analysis can promise significance, biological validation or publication. Conclusions must reflect study design, data quality and uncertainty.

How are data transfer and ownership handled?

Agree a suitable transfer method, access, data rights, code licensing and retention arrangements before sharing files. The public inquiry form accepts no uploads.
