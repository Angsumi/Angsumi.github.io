Our starter websites start at ₹2,000 fixed price with zero agency bloat. Specialized bioinformatics and data analysis consulting starts from ₹3,000 depending on dataset complexity and project scope. Every website we engineer achieves sub-second load speeds and a 100% Core Web Vitals score on mobile. We hand-code clean semantic HTML, CSS, and modern JavaScript, completely eliminating the database bloat and security vulnerabilities of 30+ slow WordPress plugins.

Yes! We specialize in tailored digital systems for Northeast India: Doctor OPD token counters & WhatsApp prescriptions, school fee payment & noticeboard portals, Assam silk & handloom direct-to-consumer stores, tea estate export catalogs, and wildlife safari booking platforms.

Yes, 100%. You retain complete legal ownership of your domain name, website source code, data analysis scripts, research results, and database. You are never locked into our services and can host or migrate your files anywhere you want.
