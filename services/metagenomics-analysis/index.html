Can I inquire before my data are ready?

Yes. Choose your current stage and describe the study question. Feasibility and planning can be scoped separately.

How does microbial composition differ across your samples?

Bring these inputs to scoping

  • State whether inputs are marker-gene amplicons or shotgun reads; provide sample type, read layout and sequencing information.
  • Provide sample metadata, groups, repeated measures, extraction batches and available negative or positive controls.
  • For amplicons, include target region and primers. For shotgun requests, discuss host reads, reference coverage and whether profiling or assembly is appropriate.

Prerequisites and interpretation limits

Taxonomic resolution depends on assay and references. Relative abundance is not an absolute cell count. Contamination can dominate low-biomass samples; missing controls constrain interpretation. Functional inference and direct measurement are distinct.
