## Modules to prioritize
Mark essential, later, or out of scope:
- Public notices and information
- Admission inquiries
- Attendance
- Fee ledger and receipts
- Timetable and assignments
- Exams and published results
- Guardian communication

## Access and data
- [ ] Define each role's permitted records and actions.
- [ ] Avoid public disclosure of student or guardian details.
- [ ] Define correction, export, retention and account removal procedures.
- [ ] Use synthetic student records for demos and tests.
- [ ] Verify payment status with the provider; a browser redirect is not payment proof.
- [ ] Agree audit history, backup and restore checks.
