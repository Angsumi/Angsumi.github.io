# Business digital-readiness assessment

Score each statement 0 (no), 1 (partial), or 2 (yes). This is a conversation guide, not a validated business score.

| Area | Question | Score |
|---|---|---|
| Customer journey | Can a new customer find accurate services and contact details? | /2 |
| Inquiry handling | Is each inquiry recorded with an owner and next action? | /2 |
| Operations | Are repeated tasks documented? | /2 |
| Data | Is there a single maintained source for essential records? | /2 |
| Access | Do staff have individual accounts and appropriate access? | /2 |
| Recovery | Is a backup restored and tested periodically? | /2 |
| Mobile | Can customers complete the main task on a phone? | /2 |
| Measurement | Do you know which inquiries become paying work? | /2 |

Do not use the total alone to decide what to buy. Circle the two gaps causing the most missed work, errors or customer friction.
