# Owner-reviewed message templates

These are drafting templates, not automated sends. Fill only with recorded facts; verify recipient, scope and permission. Only acknowledgement and owner notifications are implemented by the worker. Marketing follow-ups are disabled by default.

## Inquiry received
Subject: ANGSUMI inquiry {lead_id}
Hello {name}, we received your {category} inquiry. Your reference is {lead_id}. We will review the scope and contact you about next steps. Please do not send confidential datasets by email.

## Requirements clarification
Subject: A few questions about {lead_id}
Hello {name}, to scope your project, could you clarify {specific_missing_requirements}? We can discuss a secure transfer separately if files are needed. You do not need to share confidential data at this stage.

## Proposal
Subject: Proposed scope for {lead_id}
Hello {name}, attached is our proposed scope: {deliverables}, {acceptance_checks}, {timeline}, {price_and_currency}, {revisions}, {third_party_costs}, {support}, {exclusions}. Please review these terms before work starts. This proposal does not imply acceptance or payment.

## Follow-up
Subject: Is {lead_id} still active?
Hello {name}, is your project still active? Reply if you would like to clarify the scope. If your plans have changed, let us know and we will close the follow-up task. Promotional follow-ups require recorded opt-in and an unsubscribe route.

## Project commencement
Subject: Starting the agreed work — {lead_id}
Hello {name}, the agreed scope is {scope_version}. Our next milestone is {milestone} on {agreed_date}. Your contact is {assigned_person}. We will use {approved_transfer_route} for any restricted files.

## Delivery
Subject: Deliverables ready for review — {lead_id}
Hello {name}, the agreed deliverables are available through {authorized_delivery_route}. Please review {acceptance_checks}. The methods, versions and limitations are included in {documentation}. Let us know about issues within {agreed_review_period}.

## Feedback
Subject: Your feedback on {lead_id}
Hello {name}, did the delivered work meet the agreed requirements? We would value feedback on {specific_workflow}. We will ask separately before using your name, project or feedback in public materials.

## Repeat project
Subject: Next steps for your research or system
Hello {name}, if you have a related task, we can scope it around {relevant_context}. This outreach requires an appropriate recorded communication basis; do not send as an unsolicited campaign. Include the applicable unsubscribe route for promotional email.
