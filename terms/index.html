Terms of service

Research analysis begins with an agreed scope. Updated 8 November 2026.

Inquiries and feasibility

An inquiry starts a conversation. It is not acceptance of work, a confirmed deadline or a price commitment. Supported methods, input readiness and availability are reviewed before work is agreed.

Written project scope

Agree the research question, eligible inputs, methods, deliverables, compute costs, milestones, payment terms, revisions and acceptance checks in a written proposal. Additional analyses or changed inputs may require a revised scope.

Data, code and permissions

Provide data only through an agreed transfer route and with appropriate rights and permissions. The public inquiry form accepts no files. Ownership, licenses, handover materials, retention and reuse rights are specified in the project agreement; third-party materials remain subject to their own terms.

Interpretation and support

Analysis does not guarantee statistical significance, biological validation or publication. Outputs must be interpreted with their documented assumptions and limitations. Review rounds, handover support and maintenance are agreed by scope.

Existing agreements

Existing signed project agreements govern the work covered by those agreements. This page does not replace their negotiated terms.
